Application for Scholarship

Apply for a Ministry scholarship to pursue higher education at an approved university or institution outside Kuwait.

About this service

Apply electronically for a Ministry scholarship to pursue higher education through an approved scholarship plan.

What you can do

  • Submit a new scholarship application
  • Select preferred study destinations and majors
  • Upload required documents
  • Track your application status
  • Respond to requests for additional information or documents

Eligibility

Eligibility depends on the scholarship plan and academic programme selected.

  • Kuwaiti citizenship, where applicable
  • Required secondary-school qualification
  • Required academic percentage
  • Age requirements
  • Required academic/standardized tests where applicable
  • Must meet the conditions of the selected scholarship plan

Plan-specific requirements will be listed here.

Required documents

Basic documents required for all countries:

  • Kuwaiti Passport

    A valid Kuwaiti passport.

  • Birth Certificate

    Student’s birth certificate (In Arabic and does not need translation).

  • Secondary School Grades

    Stage Grade Detection Secondary school for four years from grade nine to grade twelve in English.

  • Visa Scholarship

    Visa Scholarships for those admitted to foreign countries.

  • Medical Examination Form

    It is extracted from the academic system under the item (Completing the Admission Data)

  • Vaccination Certificate

    Complete Immunization Certificate (extracted from the Preventive Health Center).

  • TOEFL/IELTS Result

    International TOEFL (minimum score of 45) or an academic IELTS test (minimum score of 5) from a center approved by the Ministry within the State of Kuwait only, within one year of the admission results and prior to joining the mission headquarters.
